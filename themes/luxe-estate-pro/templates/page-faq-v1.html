Buying

How do I get started buying a luxury property in Miami? +

The first step is a complimentary consultation with one of our senior advisors. We'll discuss your goals, lifestyle preferences, budget, and timeline to develop a personalized search strategy. We also recommend getting pre-approved for financing before beginning your search so you can act quickly when the right property is found.

Do you have access to off-market properties? +

Yes. A significant portion of luxury transactions in Miami occur off-market. Our network of relationships with sellers, developers, and attorneys gives our clients exclusive first access to properties before they are publicly listed. This is one of our most valued advantages.

How long does it typically take to find and close on a home? +

The search period varies by client — some find their property within weeks, while others prefer a longer, more selective process. Once under contract, luxury transactions typically close in 30–45 days. Our average from offer acceptance to closing is 21 days for clients who are pre-approved and decisive.

Selling

How do you market luxury properties? +

Our luxury marketing program includes professional photography and videography, drone footage, 3D virtual tours, editorial-quality print materials, digital campaigns targeting ultra-HNW buyers, and placement in premium publications including Mansion Global, The Wall Street Journal, and Robb Report. We also leverage our global buyer network directly.

What is your commission structure? +

Our commission structure is competitive and transparent. We are happy to discuss specifics during your initial consultation. Our results consistently demonstrate that our marketing investment and negotiation expertise more than compensate for our fees through higher sale prices and faster closings.

Financing

What financing options are available for luxury properties? +

Luxury property financing includes conventional jumbo loans, portfolio loans, asset-backed loans, and private banking financing. We work with preferred mortgage partners who specialize in high-net-worth clients and can structure creative financing solutions. Many of our clients also purchase with cash or utilize 1031 exchanges.
