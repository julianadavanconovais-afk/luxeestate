Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started buying a luxury property in Miami? +

The first step is a complimentary consultation with one of our senior advisors. We'll discuss your goals, lifestyle preferences, budget, and timeline to develop a personalized search strategy. We also recommend getting pre-approved for financing before beginning your search so you can act quickly when the right property is found.

Do you have access to off-market properties? +

Yes. A significant portion of luxury transactions in Miami occur off-market. Our network of relationships with sellers, developers, and attorneys gives our clients exclusive first access to properties before they are publicly listed.

How long does it typically take to close on a home? +

Once under contract, luxury transactions typically close in 30–45 days. Our average from offer acceptance to closing is 21 days for clients who are pre-approved and decisive. The search period before finding the right property varies by client preference.

How do you market luxury properties for sale? +

Our luxury marketing program includes professional photography and videography, drone footage, 3D virtual tours, editorial-quality print materials, digital campaigns targeting ultra-HNW buyers, and placement in Mansion Global, WSJ, and Robb Report. We also leverage our global buyer network directly.

Can international buyers purchase property in Miami? +

Yes, foreign nationals can purchase US real estate. There are FIRPTA withholding requirements and specific financing considerations for non-resident buyers. Our international specialists — including multilingual advisors — guide buyers through every aspect of purchasing from abroad, including remote closings.

What financing options are available? +

Luxury property financing includes conventional jumbo loans, portfolio loans, asset-backed loans, and private banking financing. We work with preferred mortgage partners who specialize in high-net-worth clients. Many of our clients also purchase with cash or utilize 1031 exchanges.

What is your commission structure? +

Our commission structure is competitive and transparent. We discuss specifics during your initial consultation. Our results consistently demonstrate that our marketing investment and negotiation expertise more than compensate through higher sale prices and faster closings.
