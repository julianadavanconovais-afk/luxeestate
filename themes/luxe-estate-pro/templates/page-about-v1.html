How It All Began

15 Years of Defining
Miami Luxury

Founded in 2008 by James Thornton with a singular vision — to bring genuine expertise, white-glove service, and unwavering integrity to Miami's luxury real estate market. What began as a boutique operation with three agents has grown into the city's most trusted luxury brokerage.

Today, Luxe Estate represents over $2.4 billion in completed transactions, a team of 40+ seasoned professionals, and a client base that spans six continents. Our reputation is built on one promise: to deliver extraordinary results with exceptional service.
