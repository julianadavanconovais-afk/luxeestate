The Neighborhood

Coral Gables — "The City Beautiful" — is one of the most distinguished residential communities in the United States. Developed in the 1920s by George Merrick with a unified Mediterranean Revival architectural vision.

Today, Coral Gables attracts international executives, diplomats, and discerning families who prize its walkable Village area, excellent private schools, and proximity to downtown Miami while retaining its village-like ambiance.
