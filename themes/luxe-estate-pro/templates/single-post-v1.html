Miami's Luxury Market Reaches New Heights: Q1 2026 Report

Sales volume in Miami's $3M+ segment surged 34% year-over-year in Q1 2026, driven by continued migration from New York, California, and international buyers seeking trophy properties.

James Thornton

CEO & Founder · Luxe Estate

Share Save

Miami's luxury real estate market continued its extraordinary run into 2026, with the first quarter delivering numbers that exceeded even the most bullish analyst forecasts. Transactions in the $3 million and above segment totaled 312 — a 34% increase over Q1 2025 — with total dollar volume crossing $1.8 billion for the quarter alone.

The Migration Effect

At the heart of this surge is the ongoing migration of high-net-worth individuals from high-tax states and major metropolitan areas. New York and California residents accounted for 41% of all luxury transactions in Q1, drawn to Florida's zero income tax, favorable legal environment, and unmatched quality of life.

"We have never seen demand like this. Properties that were considered aspirational two years ago now routinely close at or above asking price within days."

— James Thornton, CEO

Neighborhood Breakdown

Coral Gables led all neighborhoods with 78 transactions averaging $4.2M, while Miami Beach's waterfront sector set a new quarterly record with 12 transactions above $10M. Coconut Grove saw a 52% volume increase as buyers seek larger lots with mature canopy.

Looking Ahead

With inventory remaining historically tight and buyer demand showing no signs of cooling, we expect Q2 2026 to deliver another record-breaking quarter. The pipeline of international buyers — particularly from Latin America and Southeast Asia — has never been deeper.
