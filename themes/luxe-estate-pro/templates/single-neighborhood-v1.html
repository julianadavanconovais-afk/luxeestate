Neighborhood Overview

Coral Gables — "The City Beautiful" — is one of the most distinguished residential communities in the United States. Developed in the 1920s by George Merrick with a unified Mediterranean Revival architectural vision, the neighborhood's wide boulevards, ornate fountains, and historic Biltmore Hotel convey an elegance unmatched in South Florida.

Today, Coral Gables attracts international executives, diplomats, and discerning families who prize its walkable Village area, excellent private schools, and proximity to downtown Miami and the airport while retaining its village-like ambiance.
