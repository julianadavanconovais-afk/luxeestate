About This Property

Experience the pinnacle of Miami luxury living in this spectacular full-floor penthouse atop one of Brickell's most prestigious towers. Commanding 360-degree panoramic views of Biscayne Bay, Miami's glittering skyline, and the Atlantic Ocean, this extraordinary residence redefines sophisticated urban living.

The meticulously designed interior features soaring 12-foot ceilings, white Italian marble floors, and walls of floor-to-ceiling glass that blur the boundary between interior and exterior. The chef's kitchen is appointed with Sub-Zero appliances, custom cabinetry, and a waterfall island of Calacatta marble.

The primary suite occupies its own private wing, offering a spa-caliber bathroom with freestanding soaking tub, walk-in rain shower, and his-and-her custom closets. A private 1,200 sqft wraparound terrace provides alfresco entertaining space like no other.
