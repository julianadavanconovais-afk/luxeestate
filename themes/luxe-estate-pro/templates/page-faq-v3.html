How do I get started buying a luxury property in Miami? +

The first step is a complimentary consultation with one of our senior advisors. We'll discuss your goals, lifestyle preferences, budget, and timeline to develop a personalized search strategy. Getting pre-approved for financing before your search ensures you can move decisively.

Do you have access to off-market properties? +

Yes. A significant portion of luxury transactions in Miami occur off-market. Our relationships with sellers, developers, and attorneys give clients exclusive first access to properties before public listing. This is one of our most valued advantages.

How quickly can I close on a luxury property? +

Once under contract, luxury transactions typically close in 30–45 days. Our average from offer acceptance to closing is 21 days for pre-approved, decisive buyers. Cash transactions can close in as few as 7–14 days.

Can international buyers purchase remotely? +

Absolutely. We facilitate remote purchases for international clients regularly. Our multilingual advisors guide buyers through FIRPTA requirements, financing options for non-residents, and coordinate virtual tours, digital document signing, and international closings.

What financing options are available? +

Options include conventional jumbo loans, portfolio loans, asset-backed loans, and private banking financing. We work with preferred partners specializing in high-net-worth clients. Many clients purchase with cash or use 1031 exchanges. Use our mortgage calculator to estimate payments.
